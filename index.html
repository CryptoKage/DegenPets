// System_Core: What is Degen Pets?

This is an experimental simulation built on the blockchain, merging strategic pet management with the logic of automated trading. Collect unique Pet NFTs hard-coded with distinct strategies and verifiable on-chain parameters. Deploy them in a simulated cyber-market, manage their operational efficiency (Mood), and compete based on quantifiable performance.

// $DGPT_Protocol: Ecosystem Fuel

$DGPT powers Degen Pets. Earned in Phase 1 (PvE) via performance, it's essential for acquiring pets, forging evolution, accessing the Quant Lab, enhancing pets with items, and entering high-stakes tournaments. Strong burn mechanics counter emissions. Initial liquidity is system-generated, sustained by automated NFT royalty buybacks.
