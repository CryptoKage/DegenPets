## 3.1 Pet NFTs: Unique & Verifiable Assets

*   **Standard:** ERC721A for gas-efficient ownership management.
*   **On-Chain Traits:** Key metadata including Species ID, unique Strategy Parameters are stored directly on the blockchain, ensuring transparency and immutability.
*   **Provable Scarcity:** Each Pet species has a maximum supply determined by the number of unique parameter combinations possible, guaranteeing verifiable rarity across the ~30,000 total initial Pet supply. *(Note: A small number of the highest supply species may share parameters near the supply cap)*.
