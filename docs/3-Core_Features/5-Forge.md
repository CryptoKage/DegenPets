## 3.5 Forging: Evolution & Supply Sink

*   **Core Loop:** Once unlocked, Players can select two owned Degen Pets NFTs, pay a forging fee in DGPT, and permanently burn both NFTs.
*   **Cybernetic NFT:** A Cybernetic NFT is minted. The selected pets strageies and values are then stored with the players address.
*   **Feed the algo:** Burnt stratagies are not lost and are kept track of, enhancing the quants effect by allowing the player to access more parameters with their choosen strategy. 
*   **Purpose:** Acts as a NFT supply sink, a $DGPT burn mechanism, and rewards players as the game evolves to incorporate a simulated defi landscape.
*   **Evolution:** Cybernetic NFTs represent the end game objective, either granting the player access to v2 of the game (dependent on degenpets success) or when the LP timelock ends Cybernetic NFT holders are the receipiants of 40% LP  DGPT/APE   (60% used to reinvest in LP/Cover project debts/Buy burn) (Decisions will be clearly communicated).
