## 3.3 Badges & Achievements

*   **Rewarding Milestones:** A comprehensive system of non-transferable badges recognizes both player achievements (collecting pets, tournament success, PnL goals) and individual pet accomplishments (trading streaks, volume, specific trade types like "Diamond Hands").
*   **Incentives:** Most badges grant a small, one-time $DGPT reward (Player: 10 Pet: 5), funded by a dedicated reward pool. *(See Tokenomics for pool details)*.
