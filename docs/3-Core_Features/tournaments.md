## 3.6 Tournaments: Competitive Playground

*   **Regular Events:** Weekly free-entry Standard Tournaments ranking players by Net PnL ($DGPT earned - lost on closed trades during the week), including a "Rekt Royale" bonus for the lowest performer. Prizes funded from a dedicated Tournament Pool*.
*   **High Stakes:** Monthly Major Tournaments requiring a $DGPT Buy-in Fee (25* $DGPT). 90%* of collected fees fund the prize pool for that event, and 10%* are burned.
*   **Variety:** Rotating special events with unique rules (Species Showdowns, Timeframe Challenges, Market Madness, Consistency Challenge) to keep competition fresh.
*   **Community:** Optional representation of partner communities on leaderboards for players holding verified partner NFTs.
