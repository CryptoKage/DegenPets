## 3.5 Forging: Evolution & Supply Sink

*   **Core Loop:** Once unlocked, Players can select two owned Pet NFTs, pay a forging fee in DGPT, and permanently burn both pet NFTs.
*   **Cybernetic NFT:** A Cybernetic NFT is minted. The selected pets strageies and values are then stored with the players address.
*   **Submit to the algo:** Burnt stratagies are not lost and are kept track of, gen2 assets will ONLY be accessable through the cybernetic NFT. 
*   **Purpose:** Acts as a NFT supply sink, a $DGPT burn mechanism, and rewards players as the game evolves to incorporate a simulated defi landscape.
