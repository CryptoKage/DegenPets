## 4.6 Liquidity & Sustainability

*   **Initial Liquidity:** The primary DGPT/ETH liquidity pool is established automatically using 90%* of the ETH proceeds from each Trading Desk purchase, paired with $DGPT from the dedicated Initial LP Allocation (5%* of total supply). This ensures baseline liquidity from day one.
*   **Ongoing Liquidity Support & Buy Pressure:** A 5%* royalty fee is applied to all secondary sales of Degen Pet NFTs on supported marketplaces. This fee is automatically processed:
    *   1%* goes directly to the Project Treasury (in ETH/stablecoin) for operational funding.
    *   4%* is used to market-buy $DGPT from the DGPT/ETH LP. This purchased $DGPT, along with the equivalent value in ETH/stablecoin from the fee, is then added back into the liquidity pool. This mechanism creates constant buy pressure funded by market activity and systematically increases LP depth over time.
*   **Project Funding:** The project operates on a lean model, funded initially by 10%* of ETH from Trading Desk sales and sustained by the ongoing 1%* NFT royalty fee.
