## 4.1 Overview
The Degen Pets ecosystem contains a variety of NFTs and Tokens, trading fees and royalties of which are sent to a smart contract to either buy, burn, strengthen lp, strengthen bid depth on its own assets. 

$DGPT is an ERC20 utility token central to the Degen Pets experience. It functions as the primary reward mechanism, the currency for essential in-game actions, and a key component of the ecosystem's economic loops designed for sustainability and long-term engagement.

Pet NFTs are minted with $DGPT and are crucial in aquiring more (in PvE or PvP mode).

Founders Keys are NFTs representing ownership of the parent company of Degen Pets, used to raise funds and decentralize ownership.

Apegen Card NFTs and other existing assets can be used in the Forge to incentive reduction in supply.
