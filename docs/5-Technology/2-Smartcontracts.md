A simplified list of the contracts and their purpose

* $DGPT Token Contract: This is the official contract for the game's token, $DGPT. It keeps track of everyone's $DGPT balance.
* Degen Pet NFT Contract: This is the official contract for the games Pet NFTs. It stores the basic info for each pet, like its species and strategy variables.
* DegenPetsApegenCard Contract: This is the official contract for the games promotional NFTs. Usable in the forge to claim tokens. 
* Trading Desk Contract: The first stop for participation. Pay a small fee in APE coin [30 APE] to get access to the trading desk and start your journey. (Comes with free pet mint)
* Pet Adoption Contract: Use 100 $DGPT tokens here to mint a new pet. The $DGPT spent gets burned.
* Pet Forge Contract: Bring two Degen Pets here, pay a $DGPT fee, and the forge will burn your two pets and give you a special "Cybernetic" pass NFT in return!
* Tournament Manager Contract: Pay the $DGPT entry fee for special tournaments here. A small part of the fee is burned, and the rest is held securely to be used for tournament prizes later.
* Reward Pool Contracts (PvE, Achievement, Tournament): These are like secure vaults holding the $DGPT rewards you can earn from playing the game (trading, completing achievements, winning tournaments). The team uses these to send out prizes.
* LP Token Locker Contract: When the game earns money from NFT royalties, some of it is used to support the $DGPT token on trading sites (DEXs). This contract holds the "receipts" (LP Tokens) from that support securely for a long time (3 years).
* Royalty Processor Contract: This smart helper automatically collects ETH royalties from NFT sales, swaps some for DGPT,addssupport(DGPT,addssupport(DGPT and APE) on camelot(dex), and sends the receipts to the LP Token Locker.
