# 1. Introduction: Introduction & Vision

At its core, Degen Pets is an experiment. We set out to explore the intersection of gameplay and cryptocurrency trading. To us crypto is a game, a good presale, trading various assets and briefly associating with like minded indiciduals on the timeline. Whilt working on automated backtesting strategies we wondered if we could blend pet management mechanics with the world of automated cryptocurrency trading strategies, with pets trading against the simulated market (PvE Mode) until the smart contract is depleted of tokens then switching to an open order book and trade against each other (PvP Mode).

Degen Pets is built on the following pillars:

-Make something we find interesting. [A game style experiance based around real trading stragies with the potential to expand beyond simulated markets.] -Deploy with puropse, anything created should have a plan of use beyond just speculation. [Protocol rebuys and strengthens its liquidity across assets where possible. 'See Royalty Processor Contact'] -Stong links build strong chains. [Elevating and promoting other projects and artists onchain benefits us all, include other projects and reward users chain history]

We believe that blockchain games in themselves are metagames wherby the interaction of assets put out by the project in themselves can be forms of game loops. When building the project we thought about each type of player, from the speculator trading the token, player grinding the game or the investor holding founder keys.
