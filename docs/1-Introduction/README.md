# 1. Introduction: Introduction & Vision

At its core, Degen Pets is an experiment. We set out to explore the intersection of gameplay and cryptocurrency trading. To us crypto is in iteself a metagame, a wallets interaction with assets over a given time effects its total balance. Whilt working on automated backtesting strategies we wondered if we could blend pet management mechanics with the world of automated cryptocurrency trading strategies, with pets trading against the simulated market (PvE Mode) until the smart contract is depleted of tokens then switching to an open order book and trade against each other (PvP Mode).
