## Guide: Your Goal & Getting Started

Welcome to Degen Pets! This guide will walk you through playing the game.

### What's the Goal?

While having Pets achieve consistently profitable trades is rewarding, Degen Pets offers multiple paths to success and earning the $DGPT utility token. The core objective is to strategically manage your collection of unique Pet NFTs to maximize their potential, whether through consistent wins, enduring losses, or specific achievements.

*   **Profitability:** Finding the right Pet (Species + Parameters) and matching it to the ideal Market/Timeframe can lead to significant $DGPT rewards from profitable trades (especially those with high profit margins). Competing for the top spots on PnL leaderboards in Tournaments is a primary goal for competitive players.
*   **Badge Hunting:** Every Pet, winner or loser, contributes. The **Badge system** rewards various milestones and streaks – both positive and negative. Pets enduring long losing streaks can earn substantial $DGPT via the "Black Clover" badge*, potentially funding the acquisition of new pets or Forging attempts. Maximizing badge collection across your roster is a valid and rewarding strategy. (See the full [Badge List](Appendices/Badge_List.md) for details).
*   **Collection & Forging:** Underperforming Gen 0 / Gen 1 pets have value. Through **Forging**, you can burn two pets (and pay a $DGPT fee*) to create a new, potentially improved Pet NFT. Furthermore, Forging is planned as the gateway to future **Generation 2 Pets** which may unlock entirely different gameplay mechanics (like LP management or arbitrage simulation*) – making even "losing" pets valuable components in your long-term strategy.

Ultimately, success means engaging with the simulation, understanding your pets' strengths and weaknesses, and utilizing the game's mechanics (Trading, Mood, Items, Forging, Tournaments, Badges) to grow your collection and $DGPT balance.

### Getting Started: Your First Steps

1.  **Connect Your Wallet:** Click the "[Connect Wallet]" button on the main site. Use a standard EVM-compatible wallet (like MetaMask, Rabby, etc.). Approve the connection request in your wallet.
2.  **Check for Trading Desk:** The application will automatically check if your connected wallet already owns Trading Desk access.
3.  **Purchase Trading Desk (If Needed):**
    *   If you don't have access, a pop-up or prompt will appear asking you to purchase the Trading Desk for **0.01 ETH***.
    *   *(Partner Bonus Check):* At this stage, the system checks (via off-chain snapshot*) if your wallet holds a designated NFT from a partner project.
    *   Click "[Buy Trading Desk]" and approve the transaction in your wallet. Remember, 90%* of this ETH goes to the $DGPT/ETH Liquidity Pool, and 10%* supports project operations.
4.  **Receive Your First Pet:**
    *   Upon successful purchase, you'll receive your first Pet NFT!
    *   If you held a verified partner NFT, you'll get to *choose* between the partner's specific Pet Species or a random one.
    *   If you didn't hold a partner NFT, you'll receive a Pet of a random Species.
    *   *Crucially:* Even if you choose a species, its trading **parameters** are randomly generated, making your pet unique.
