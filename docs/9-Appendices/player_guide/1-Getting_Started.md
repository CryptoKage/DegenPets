# Guide: Your Goal & Getting Started

Welcome to Degen Pets! This guide will walk you through playing the game, starting with understanding your objectives and taking your first steps.

## What's the Goal Here?

Degen Pets offers more than just trying to make simulated profit. While guiding your Pets to successful trades is a core part of the game and a primary way to earn the $DGPT utility token, there are multiple interconnected goals and strategies you can pursue:

1.  **Optimize Trading Performance:**
    *   **The Challenge:** Each Pet NFT has a unique strategy (based on its Species) modified by unique, random parameters. Your task is to figure out which **Market** (e.g., BTC/USDT, ETH/USDT) and which **Timeframe** (e.g., 15m, 1h, 4h) allows *your specific Pet* to perform best.
    *   **The Reward:** Pets that consistently close profitable trades earn more $DGPT, especially those achieving large wins (triggering profit bonuses*). Top performers climb the Leaderboards and dominate Tournaments. Using the [Visual Backtester](Markets_Simulation.md#visual-backtester-tool) (requires unlock*) is key here.

2.  **Master the Badge System:**
    *   **The Strategy:** Don't discount your underperformers! The Badge system rewards a wide range of activities and outcomes for both your account (Player Badges) and individual Pets (Pet Badges).
    *   **The Reward:** Achieving badges grants one-time $DGPT rewards*. Notably, Pets enduring long losing streaks can earn the "Black Clover" badge*, potentially providing enough $DGPT to help acquire new Pets or fund Forging attempts. Actively "hunting" specific badges by putting pets in different situations can be a rewarding strategy in itself. Check the full [Badge List](../Badge_List.md) for targets!

3.  **Evolve Your Collection via Forging:**
    *   **The Long Game:** Even pets that consistently lose or seem suboptimal for current markets hold value. They are essential components for **Forging**. By burning two parent Pets and paying a $DGPT fee*, you create a new Pet NFT.
    *   **The Reward:** The offspring inherits the Species/Strategy of the more consistent parent (based on Win Rate*) and gets new parameters (with a small chance* to inherit good ones). This allows you to potentially consolidate value and strategically aim for species you prefer. Forging is also the planned pathway towards future **Generation 2 Pets**, which may unlock entirely new gameplay mechanics like simulated LP provision or arbitrage*.

**In summary:** Your overall goal is to engage deeply with the Degen Pets ecosystem. Experiment with market assignments, manage your pets' Mood using [Items](../Item_List.md), collect diverse Pets, strategically pursue Badges, compete in [Tournaments](8-Tournaments.md), and utilize [Forging](6-Forging_Guide.md) and the [Quant Lab](7-Quant_Lab_Guide.md) to build the most effective and accomplished roster according to *your* preferred playstyle.

## Getting Started: Your First Steps

Ready to dive in? Here's how to get your operation running:

1.  **Connect Your Wallet:** Visit the Degen Pets application website. Click the prominent `[Connect Wallet]` button. Choose your preferred EVM-compatible wallet (e.g., MetaMask, Rabby, WalletConnect option) and approve the connection request in the wallet pop-up.

2.  **Check for Trading Desk Access:** Once connected, the application automatically verifies if your wallet address has already purchased Trading Desk access.

3.  **Purchase Trading Desk (If Required):**
    *   If this is your first time, you'll likely see a pop-up or prompt indicating you need Trading Desk access.
    *   **(Partner Bonus Check):** The system simultaneously checks if your wallet holds a designated NFT from one of our official partner projects (based on periodic snapshots* - allow up to 12 hours* for new acquisitions to register).
    *   The cost is **0.01 ETH***. Click the `[Buy Trading Desk]` or similar button.
    *   Your wallet will ask you to confirm the transaction (sending 0.01 ETH). Approve it. Remember, 90%* of this ETH funds the $DGPT/ETH liquidity pool, supporting the token's ecosystem, while 10%* goes to project operations.

4.  **Receive Your First Pet NFT:**
    *   As soon as your ETH transaction confirms on the blockchain, the Trading Desk access is granted to your account.
    *   You will immediately receive your first Degen Pet NFT!
    *   **If Partner NFT Was Held:** You will be presented with a choice: select the Pet Species associated with the partner project, OR opt for a random Pet.
    *   **If No Partner NFT Held:** You will automatically receive a Pet of a random Species.
    *   **Uniqueness:** No matter how you receive it, your Pet's specific trading parameters are randomly generated within its species' rules, making it unique! It will appear in your Collection.

5.  **Explore Your Dashboard/Collection:** You should now have access to the main application interface. Congratulations, you're officially a Degen Pets manager!
