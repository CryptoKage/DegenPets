# Guide: Understanding Your Pets (Collection & Details)

Your Pet NFTs are the heart of Degen Pets. Managing your collection effectively and understanding each pet's unique characteristics is crucial for success. This section covers viewing your full roster and diving into the specifics of a single pet.

## Navigating the Collection Screen

The Collection screen provides an overview of *all* the Degen Pet NFTs associated with your connected wallet.

### Key Features:

1.  **Display Grid/List:** Your pets are typically displayed as a series of **Pet Cards**. Each card provides a quick summary:
    *   Pet Image/Icon*
    *   Name / ID#
    *   Species
    *   Current Mood (Visual Bar/Icon)
    *   Status (Idle, Active [Market/TF], Sleeping)
    *   *(Optional: Small indicators for notable Badges)*
2.  **Filtering & Sorting:** Look for options to filter your collection (e.g., by Species, Status) and sort it (e.g., by Mood, ID#, Name, Generation*). This is essential for managing larger collections.
3.  **Assign to Desk:** Each Pet Card for an *inactive* pet should have a clear button or interaction (like clicking the card) that allows you to `[Assign to Trading Desk]`. If your desk is full (4 active pets), you might be prompted to choose which active pet to replace.
4.  **Navigation:** Clicking on a specific Pet Card (often on the name or image area) will typically navigate you to that Pet's dedicated Detail View.

The Collection is your central hub for organizing your roster and selecting which pets to activate.

## The Pet Detail View

When you select a specific pet from the Collection or the Trading Desk, you'll arrive at its Detail View. This screen contains *all* available information and actions related to that single Pet NFT.

### Information Displayed:

1.  **Primary Identification:**
    *   Large Pet Image/Representation*
    *   Pet Name / ID#
    *   Species Name
    *   Generation* (Increases with successful Forging)
    *   Owner Wallet Address (Yours)
    *   Link to view NFT on Blockchain Explorer / Marketplace*.
2.  **Current Status & Mood:**
    *   **Status:** Clearly displayed (e.g., `Active - Trading BTC/USDT on 1h`, `Sleeping - 2h 45m remaining`, `Idle in Collection`).
    *   **Mood:** Detailed view of the Mood bar and the exact numerical value (e.g., `Mood: 78/100`).
3.  **Strategy & Parameters (Crucial!):**
    *   **Strategy:** Displays the core strategy tied to the Pet's Species (e.g., "EMA Cross", "MACD Trend").
    *   **Unique Parameters:** Lists the specific, immutable parameters generated for *this* Pet NFT that modify its strategy (e.g., `EMA Fast Length: 12`, `EMA Slow Length: 28` or `RSI Length: 14`, `Oversold: 25`, `Overbought: 75`). Understanding these is key to optimizing market/timeframe assignment.
4.  **Performance Statistics:**
    *   Lifetime Net PnL ($DGPT earned/lost by this pet).
    *   Win Rate % (Profitable Trades / Total Closed Trades).
    *   Total Trades Executed.
    *   Longest Winning Streak / Losing Streak.
    *   *(More stats may be added over time)*.
5.  **Earned Badges:**
    *   A dedicated section displaying the icons/names of all [Pet Badges](../Badge_List.md#pet-badges-attributes) this specific pet has earned (e.g., Green Clover, Century Trader, Diamond Hands).

### Actions Available:

Depending on the pet's current status (Active, Idle, Sleeping), you'll find relevant action buttons:

*   **`Assign to Trading Desk` / `Send to Collection`:** Toggles the pet's active status.
*   **`Assign/Change Market`:** (Only if Active or Idle on Desk) Opens the market selection interface.
*   **`Assign/Change Timeframe`:** (Only if Active or Idle on Desk) Opens the timeframe selection interface.
*   **`Use Item`:** Opens your inventory to select and use an item (like a mood booster) on this pet.
*   **`Assign to Quant Lab`:** (If Quant Lab is unlocked and slot available) Moves the pet to the Lab for strategy swapping.
*   **`Select for Forging`:** Initiates the Forging process with this pet as a potential parent.

The Pet Detail View is where you truly understand an individual pet's characteristics and manage its specific assignments and enhancements.
