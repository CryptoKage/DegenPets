# Guide: Tournaments Explained

Tournaments are regular competitive events in Degen Pets where you pit your active trading team's performance against other players for $DGPT prizes, exclusive badges, and bragging rights.

## Core Concept: PnL Competition

*   **Standard Scoring:** Unless the specific tournament rules state otherwise, your rank is determined by the **Net Profit and Loss (PnL)** achieved by your **4 active Pets** on the Trading Desk.
*   **Calculation:** PnL is calculated based on the $DGPT earned or lost from **trades closed during the official tournament window**. Unrealized gains/losses on open trades generally do not count towards the tournament score itself (though they contribute to your overall $DGPT balance).
*   **Goal:** Maximize the positive $DGPT PnL generated by your active team within the tournament duration.

## Tournament Types

Degen Pets features a variety of tournament formats:

1.  **Weekly Standard Tournament:**
    *   **Frequency:** Runs automatically every week (e.g., starts Monday 00:00 UTC, ends Sunday 23:59 UTC*).
    *   **Entry:** **Free** for all players with an active Trading Desk. Participation is automatic if you have pets trading.
    *   **Goal:** Highest Net PnL over the full week.
    *   **Prizes:** $DGPT awarded to top performers (e.g., Top 1%, Top 10%, Top 25%*) distributed from the dedicated [Tournament Prize Pool Fund](../4-Tokenomics/2-Supply_Distribution.md). Top ranks may also earn Player Badges like "Weekly Winner".
    *   **"Rekt Royale" Bonus:** A special recognition (unique Badge* + small $DGPT prize*) is awarded to the player with the *lowest* Net PnL for the week, acknowledging the unpredictable nature of trading!

2.  **Monthly Major Tournament:**
    *   **Frequency:** Runs once per month, typically for a shorter duration (e.g., 3-5 days). Dates announced in advance.
    *   **Entry:** Requires a **$DGPT Buy-in Fee** (Cost: 25* $DGPT). You must manually register and pay the fee before the tournament starts.
    *   **Goal:** Highest Net PnL over the specific tournament duration.
    *   **Prizes:** Significantly larger $DGPT prize pool compared to weekly events. The pool is primarily funded by **90%* of all collected buy-in fees**. The project may contribute additional base prizes.
    *   **Burn Mechanism:** **10%* of all collected buy-in fees are permanently burned**, adding a deflationary aspect to these high-stakes events.
    *   **Recognition:** Winners often receive exclusive "Tournament Champ" Player Badges.

3.  **Special Event Tournaments (Rotating):**
    *   **Frequency:** Run periodically, often alongside Majors or during special promotions.
    *   **Entry:** Usually **Free**.
    *   **Unique Rules:** These events change the scoring or participation criteria:
        *   *Species Showdown:* Only Pets of a specific Species contribute score.
        *   *Timeframe Challenge:* Only Pets on a specific Timeframe score.
        *   *Market Madness:* Only trades on designated assets count.
        *   *Consistency Challenge:* Ranking based on highest Win Rate % (minimum trade count required).
    *   **Prizes:** Vary per event, often $DGPT from the Tournament Pool Fund and potentially unique Badges.

## Community Representation

*   For certain tournaments (like Weekly Standard & Monthly Major), if your connected wallet holds a verified NFT from one of our partner projects, you may have the option to **"Represent"** that community.
*   This primarily adds a community tag to your name on leaderboards and allows for filtered community-specific rankings, fostering friendly competition.

## Checking Leaderboards

*   Navigate to the "Leaderboards" or "Tournaments" section of the application.
*   Select the specific tournament or leaderboard type (Weekly, Monthly, All-Time, etc.).
*   View rankings, scores (PnL), and potentially the top Pets used by leading players.

Participating in tournaments is a great way to test your pet management skills against the community and earn significant $DGPT rewards.
