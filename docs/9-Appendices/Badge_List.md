# Appendix: Badge List

Badges are non-transferable achievements awarded for reaching specific milestones or demonstrating particular gameplay patterns within Degen Pets. They serve as markers of progress and accomplishment for both players and their individual Pet NFTs. Most badges also grant a one-time $DGPT reward upon achievement, drawn from the dedicated Achievement Reward Pool.

**Note:** All $DGPT reward values marked with * are initial placeholders and subject to final balancing based on economic modeling and testing. Trigger conditions (like streak counts or PnL amounts) marked with * are also subject to tuning.

| **Black Clover**    | Achieve 25* consecutive losing closed trades.                                        | 5* $DGPT      | Indicates a significant losing streak. Resets on non-losing trade.        |
| **Century Trader**  | Complete 100* trades (win, loss, or BE).                                             | 5* $DGPT      | Milestone for activity.                                                   |
| **Millennium Trader** | Complete 1000* trades (win, loss, or BE).                                            | 5* $DGPT      | Major milestone for activity.                                             |
| **Paper Hands**     | Close a trade at a loss within 3* candles/periods of entry (occurs 3* times).        | 5* $DGPT      | Marks a tendency to cut losses quickly. *(Period based on pet's timeframe)* |
| **Diamond Hands**   | Hold a trade open for >24* candles/periods & close for >=25%* profit (occurs 3* times).| 5* $DGPT      | Marks a tendency to hold winners. *(Period based on pet's timeframe)*      |

| **Whale Rider**     | Close a single trade earning the maximum high profit bonus (+5* $DGPT bonus currently).| 5* $DGPT      | Celebrates a significant winning trade relative to entry.                 |
