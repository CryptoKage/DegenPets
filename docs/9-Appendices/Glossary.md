## Glossary

*(Define key terms used throughout the whitepaper for clarity)*

*   **$DGPT:** The native ERC20 utility token of the Degen Pets ecosystem.
*   **Active Pet:** A Pet NFT assigned to one of the 4 slots on the Trading Desk, participating in automated trading.
*   **Badges:** Non-transferable achievements awarded to players or pets for milestones.
*   **Collection:** The player's full roster of owned Pet NFTs.
*   **Forging (f.k.a. Breeding):** The process of burning two parent Pet NFTs and $DGPT to mint a new offspring Pet NFT.
*   **Market:** A specific crypto asset pair (e.g., BTC/USDT) that pets can be assigned to trade.
*   **Mood:** A simulated state reflecting a pet's recent performance, affecting its activity.
*   **Parameter:** Unique numerical values stored on each Pet NFT that modify its core trading strategy.
*   **Pet NFT:** An ERC721A non-fungible token representing a unique Degen Pet with specific traits.
*   **Phase 1 (PvE):** The initial game phase where players earn $DGPT from a reward pool by trading against the simulated market.
*   **Phase 2 (PvP):** The later game phase activated after the PvE pool depletes, where pets trade against each other using player-funded $DGPT.
*   **Quant Lab:** An endgame feature allowing temporary strategy swapping for one pet.
*   **Species:** The type of Degen Pet, determining its core trading strategy logic (e.g., Cat, Ape, Blob).
*   **Timeframe:** The candle interval (e.g., 15m, 1h) selected by the player for their pet's strategy calculation.
*   **Trading Desk:** The account-bound access pass required to play, allowing up to 4 active pets.
*   **UPP (Unrealized Profit Points):** Small $DGPT rewards earned periodically while holding an open profitable trade.
*   **VRF (Verifiable Random Function):** Chainlink service used for provably fair randomness in Forging.
