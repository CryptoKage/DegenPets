## 2.8 Earning $DGPT: Rewards for Performance

$DGPT is the lifeblood of the Degen Pets economy, earned primarily through successful gameplay during Phase 1 (PvE):
*   **Trade Closure:** Earn $DGPT for every closed trade, with rewards heavily weighted towards profitability (Loss: 0*, BE: 1*, Profit: 3* + Bonus* up to +5* for large wins).
*   **Holding Winners (UPP):** Earn small amounts of $DGPT periodically (+0.5* per 4h* interval*) for Pets holding open profitable trades.
*   **Peak Performance:** Receive a $DGPT bonus (30*) when a Pet reaches maximum Mood.
*   **Achievements:** Earn one-time $DGPT rewards (Player: 10*, Pet: 5*) for completing various Player and Pet Badges.
*   **Competition:** Win $DGPT prizes from participating in and winning Tournaments.
