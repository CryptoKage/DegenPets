## 2.1 Getting Started: Your Trading Desk

Entry into the Degen Pets ecosystem begins with acquiring your personal "Trading Desk". This is an account-bound access pass purchased for **0.01 ETH***. This initial purchase serves two key purposes: 90%* of the ETH automatically seeds the liquidity pool for our native token, $DGPT, on a decentralized exchange, while the remaining 10%* contributes to the project's operational treasury.

Upon purchasing your Trading Desk, you receive your very first Degen Pet NFT. By default, this is a randomly selected Pet from any available species. However, if your wallet holds a verified NFT from one of our official partner projects at the time of purchase (verified via periodic off-chain snapshots* - updates may take up to 12 hours*), you will be given the choice to receive a Pet corresponding to that partner's designated species or opt for the standard random pet.
