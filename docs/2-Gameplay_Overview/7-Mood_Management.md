## 2.7 Mood Management: The Trader's Temperament

Your Pets aren't just algorithms; they have simulated personalities reflected by their Mood.
*   **Impact:** Closing a trade at a loss decreases Mood (-4* Mood), while closing a profitable trade grants a small Mood boost (+1.75* Mood). Break-even trades have no mood impact.
*   **Consequences:** If a Pet's Mood drops to zero, it becomes overwhelmed and enters a mandatory **Sleep** period for 6* hours, becoming inactive on the Trading Desk. Upon waking, its Mood resets to a neutral 50*. Sleeping pets cannot trade.
*   **Intervention:** Players can actively manage Mood using items purchased with $DGPT (Energy Drinks*, Stimulants*, Relaxants*) to keep their top performers active or recover pets from low moods faster. Inactive pets in the Collection regenerate mood slowly over time (+2* Mood per hour*).
