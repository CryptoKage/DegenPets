Here's a simplified list of the contracts and their purpose

* $DGPT Token Contract: This is the official contract for our game's special money, $DGPT! It keeps track of everyone's $DGPT balance.
* Degen Pet NFT Contract: This contract proves you own your unique Degen Pet digital collectible (your NFT). It stores the basic info for each pet, like its species and generation.
* Trading Desk Contract: Your first stop! Pay a small fee in APE coin here to get your very first starter Degen Pet NFT and join the fun.
* Pet Adoption Contract: Want a specific type of basic Degen Pet? Use your $DGPT tokens here to adopt one! The $DGPT spent here gets burned (removed forever).
* Pet Forge Contract: Feeling experimental? Bring two Degen Pets here, pay a $DGPT fee (which gets burned), and the forge will burn your two pets and give you a special "Cybernetic" pass NFT in return!
* Tournament Manager Contract: Pay the $DGPT entry fee for special tournaments here. A small part of the fee is burned, and the rest is held securely to be used for tournament prizes later.
* Reward Pool Contracts (PvE, Achievement, Tournament): These are like secure vaults holding the $DGPT rewards you can earn from playing the game (trading, completing achievements, winning tournaments). The team uses these to send out prizes.
* LP Token Locker Contract: When the game earns money from NFT royalties, some of it is used to support the $DGPT token on trading sites (DEXs). This contract holds the "receipts" (LP Tokens) from that support securely for a long time (3 years) to show commitment.
* Royalty Processor Contract: This smart helper automatically collects ETH royalties from NFT sales, swaps some for DGPT,addssupport(DGPT,addssupport(DGPT and APE) on camelot(dex), and sends the receipts to the LP Token Locker.
