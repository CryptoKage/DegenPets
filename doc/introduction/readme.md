# 1. Introduction: Welcome to the Degenerate Trading Floor

This section provides an overview of the Degen Pets project, its vision, and the audience it aims to reach.

Degen Pets is a blockchain-based (EVM) pet management simulation game where players collect unique Pet NFTs, each representing a specific automated cryptocurrency trading strategy based on real technical indicators (e.g., EMA Cross, MACD, RSI).
Every Pet NFT has unique, on-chain parameters that influence its strategy's behavior.
Players purchase a "Trading Desk" (0.01 ETH) to start, receiving one initial Pet.
They assign up to 4 active Pets to trade simulated crypto assets on player-selected markets and timeframes.
Gameplay involves managing Pet "Mood" (affected by trade outcomes, requiring Items purchased with $DGPT), optimizing Pet assignments via a visual backtester tool, and competing on leaderboards. $DGPT, the utility token, is earned primarily through profitable trades in the initial PvE phase (funded by a finite pool), completing badges, and winning tournaments.
